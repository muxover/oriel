# Oriel v1.0.0 — Home, mirroring and media

**Release date:** 2026-10-28

### Install

```text
Download oriel-1.0.0.zip from this release, unzip it on a Windows PC on the TV's network, and run Install Oriel.bat, or Install Oriel System.bat on a rooted TV
```

---

## Summary

My Android TV was slow, its screen mirroring was laggy and it had no AirPlay, so I rebuilt it with Claude over three nights. This is the only release. Oriel is made for one TV, a rooted VIEW ML43VIEWA620, and it isn't maintained: I'll only come back to it if I need something new on my own TV.

---

## What's included

### Home screen

- Your apps in rows by kind, each with its Google Play icon and a line about it, light enough for a 1 GB TV
- A launch screen behind every app, and a Now playing row while music plays

### Oriel Mirror

- AirPlay from iPhone, iPad and Mac
- Win + K from Windows on rooted TVs, while the TV stays on your Wi-Fi

### Screen saver and Now Playing

- Your team's live match, the weather and the next prayer while the TV is idle
- Music from any app or from an iPhone gets a full screen in the cover's colours, and keeps playing when you go home

### Phone remote and keyboard

- Arrows, a real mouse, typing into any app, and links and files sent to the TV
- The Oriel keyboard in English, Arabic and symbols

### Player

- Films with English or Arabic subtitles, music with cover art, and photos, from the TV or a USB drive

### Settings

- Six accent colours, clock and date styles, your city and teams, night mode, picture and sound

### Built into the system

- On a rooted TV, Oriel becomes part of the system behind a boot watchdog that undoes any change that stops the TV booting
- Restore Factory puts the system back as the maker shipped it and checks the TV starts
